## TALKING POINTS (1.5-2 minutes)

"As the OAT Test Engineer and Lead Systems Engineer on the WBDM-TST project, my role was to assess the AWS infrastructure and ensure it met all requirements for security, compliance, and resilience.

I conducted a comprehensive assessment covering three key areas:

First, I evaluated the AWS infrastructure to identify architectural gaps, security vulnerabilities, and disaster recovery limitations. This involved reviewing the entire infrastructure design against best practices and compliance requirements.

Second, I validated all Non-Functional Requirements - ensuring that security, reliability, and governance requirements were comprehensively covered and supported by evidence.

Third, I reviewed the disaster recovery strategy, enhanced the on-premise playbooks and runbooks, and proposed an improved DR solution that was evaluated as feasible for future implementation.

Beyond assessment, I delivered concrete solutions:

I designed secure data transfer architecture to ensure auditable flows between AWS, on-premise systems, and cloud destinations. This was critical for meeting compliance requirements.

I delivered actionable recommendations and detailed reports to stakeholders, enabling them to understand the gaps and the path forward.

The key outcomes were:
- Identified and addressed security and compliance gaps
- Validated all NFRs with supporting governance evidence
- Enhanced disaster recovery readiness
- Established secure integration patterns
- Achieved stakeholder alignment on the modernization approach"
