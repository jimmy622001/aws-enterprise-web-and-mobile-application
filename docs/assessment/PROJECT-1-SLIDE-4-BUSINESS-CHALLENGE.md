## TALKING POINTS (2 minutes)

"This is the WBDM-TST project at Westbrom Building Society, a bank modernizing its legacy core banking system. The modernization is happening in three stages, and we're focusing on Stage 1 today.

Stage 1 involves launching a web and mobile banking application hosted in AWS. This application needs to integrate seamlessly with their on-premise infrastructure and connect to multiple third-party services including fraud detection, the core banking system, document management, and AML/KYC compliance tools.

The bank has several critical requirements:
- The cloud environment must be robust, secure, and compliant with regulatory standards
- It must be resilient against failures with high availability
- All non-functional requirements and governance controls must be validated
- They need a reliable disaster recovery strategy for their on-premise systems
- All data flows between cloud and on-premise must be secure and auditable

This is a complex integration challenge that requires careful architecture to balance security, compliance, performance, and resilience. Stages 2 and 3 will follow with additional platforms for Savings & Loan and Mortgages."
